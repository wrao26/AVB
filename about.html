About AVB

Discretion is our signature.

AVB

AVB Imobiliária was founded on a simple premise: the best properties are rarely the loudest ones. Our clients — owners, buyers and investors across Luanda, Dubai and Lisbon — come to us for judgment as much as access.

We operate as a single team across three time zones, which means a property in Dubai is evaluated with the same rigour as one in Luanda or Lisbon. No market is treated as secondary.

Every transaction is handled personally, from first enquiry to final signature — and often, well beyond it.
